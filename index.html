Tiny Town Tactics

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy for Tiny Town Tactics explains what happens to information when you play the game, watch an optional ad, or contact its developer. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to graytcompany@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The game saves level progress, coins, village arrangements, purchases made with game coins, sound and visual settings, and an age group on your device. The age screen asks for your age in years, but saves only whether you are an adult or under 18. We do not require an account, and the game does not send this saved progress or age group to our own server. Removing the app or clearing its storage may erase this progress.

Optional rewarded ads

The installed Android and iOS versions may offer adults an optional rewarded ad for game coins. Players under 18 do not see this ad option. Watching an ad is not required to play or advance through levels. Google AdMob provides these ads. Its SDK may collect and share your device's IP address, interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views, diagnostic information, and device identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ad requests in the game ask for non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. Google's privacy practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Feedback and bug reports

Adults can choose Feedback & Ideas or Report a Bug. The game opens their email app with a draft addressed to graytcompany@gmail.com. They can review or cancel it before sending. If sent, we receive the email address and message provided, along with any game context included in the draft. We use those details to respond and improve the game. The email provider handles the message under its own privacy policy. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

Children and families

The game is intended for families, including children. Parents or guardians may contact us at graytcompany@gmail.com to ask about information submitted by email or request its deletion. Children should ask a parent or guardian before sending feedback or bug reports.

Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell game progress. Saved game progress stays on your device. The AdMob SDK may send data to Google as described above, including when the SDK operates. We keep emailed feedback only as long as needed to respond, resolve an issue, or maintain related records, and delete it on a reasonable request unless we need to retain it for a legal reason. We use reasonable safeguards for email we receive; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current version will show a new effective date. Contact graytcompany@gmail.com with questions, access requests, or deletion requests.
